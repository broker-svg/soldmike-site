How much is my Woodbridge home worth?+

It depends most on what similar homes on nearby streets sold for in the last few months. We pull those sales and send you a price range with the comparables attached. Get your home value.

When is the best time to sell in Vaughan?+

Spring, from March to early June, usually brings the most buyers in Vaughan, with a second, smaller wave in September and October. The best time for you also depends on how many similar homes are for sale; fewer listings in winter can mean less competition for yours.

Should I buy first or sell first?+

In most cases, sell first, or line up both closings for the same day. Selling first tells you exactly how much you can spend and avoids carrying two homes; if you buy first, ask your lender about a bridge loan to cover the gap.

What does it cost to sell a house in Ontario?+

The main costs are real estate commission plus 13% HST on it, legal fees, and paying out your mortgage, including any prepayment penalty. Sellers in Ontario do not pay land transfer tax. Run your numbers in the calculator.
