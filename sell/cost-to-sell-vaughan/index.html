Cost to sell, answered

How much does it cost to sell a house in Vaughan?+

The main costs are real estate commission plus 13% HST on that commission, legal fees, and paying out your mortgage, including any prepayment penalty. Sellers in Ontario do not pay land transfer tax; the buyer does.

Do sellers pay land transfer tax in Ontario?+

No. Land transfer tax is paid by the buyer. Sellers pay commission, HST on the commission, legal fees and any mortgage penalty.

Is HST charged on real estate commission?+

Yes. In Ontario, 13% HST is charged on the commission.
