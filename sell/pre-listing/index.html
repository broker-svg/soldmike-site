Your 8-step launch plan

  1. Pricing meeting

    We pull the recent sales on your street, set the list price and decide on an offer date or offers any time.

  2. Prep

    A short list of repairs, paint and decluttering, plus a staging consultation.

  3. Media day

    Toronto Property Media shoots photos, drone, video, twilight and a floor plan.

  4. Coming soon

    Your home is teased on social media, to our buyer list and to the agent network before it hits the MLS®.

  5. MLS® go-live

    The listing goes live with the full photo set and video.

  6. Launch week

    Social ads, an email to our buyer list, open houses and agent showings, with feedback after every showing.

  7. Offer review

    We compare every offer on price, deposit, conditions and closing date, not just the top number.

  8. Weekly updates

    You get an update every week until the deal is firm, then we coordinate everything through closing.
