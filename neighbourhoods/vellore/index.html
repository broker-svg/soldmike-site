Living in Vellore Village

Schools

  • Vellore Woods Public School: Public (York Region DSB) elementary school.
  • Pierre Berton Public School: Public (York Region DSB) elementary school.
  • St. Emily Catholic Elementary School: Catholic (York Catholic DSB) on Vellore Woods Boulevard.
  • Emily Carr Secondary School: Public high school on Rutherford Road.
  • Tommy Douglas Secondary School: Public high school on Major Mackenzie Drive, opened in 2015.

Parks and recreation

  • Vellore Village Community Centre: Pool with water slide, gym, fitness centre and outdoor rink on Villa Royale Avenue.
  • Vellore Woods Park: Walking paths, playground, basketball court and three ponds.
  • Matthew Park: Running track, ball diamond and tennis courts.
  • Kortright Centre for Conservation: 325 hectares of trails a short drive south.

Shopping and dining

  • Weston Road and Rutherford Road: Plazas with grocery stores, banks and restaurants.
  • Major Mackenzie Drive at Highway 400: Big-box shopping with Walmart and Home Depot.
  • Vaughan Mills: Large indoor mall at the east edge of the community.

Getting around

Vellore Village sits just west of Highway 400, with access at Rutherford Road, Major Mackenzie Drive and Teston Road. The nearest GO trains are Rutherford and Maple on the Barrie line. YRT routes connect to Vaughan Mills Terminal and on to Pioneer Village and Vaughan Metropolitan Centre stations on TTC Line 1.

What is changing

  • Barrie line GO expansion: Metrolinx is adding a second track and station upgrades at Maple GO toward all-day, two-way service.
  • Highway 427 extension and Highway 413: Ontario is moving ahead in 2026 with extending Highway 427 north and building the Highway 413 east section.
  • Cortellucci Vaughan Hospital: Opened in 2021 on Major Mackenzie Drive, Vaughan's first hospital.

School boundaries change; confirm your address with the school board before you buy.

Vellore Village questions, answered

Is Vellore Village a good place to raise a family?+

Yes. Vellore Village has a community centre with a pool and rink, parks like Vellore Woods Park and Matthew Park, public and Catholic elementary schools close by, and Tommy Douglas and Emily Carr for high school.

How do I get to downtown Toronto from Vellore Village?+

By car, take Highway 400 south toward downtown Toronto. By transit, take YRT to Vaughan Metropolitan Centre or Pioneer Village and ride TTC Line 1, or take the Barrie line GO train from Rutherford or Maple.
