Living in King City & Nobleton

Schools

  • King City Public School: Public (York Region DSB) elementary school.
  • Nobleton Public School: Public (York Region DSB) elementary school.
  • Holy Name Catholic School: Catholic (York Catholic DSB) elementary school in King City.
  • St. Mary Catholic School: Catholic (York Catholic DSB) elementary school in Nobleton.
  • King City Secondary School: Public high school on King Road serving all of King Township.
  • Villanova College: Independent Catholic school, Grades 4 to 12.
  • The Country Day School: Independent co-ed day school, founded in 1972.

Parks and recreation

  • King City Memorial Park: Ball diamonds, soccer fields, playgrounds and tennis courts.
  • Nobleton Community Centre and Arena: Ice pad, hall and meeting rooms on Old King Road.
  • Cold Creek Conservation Area: Hiking and nature trails in King Township.
  • King City Trail: Valley trail along the Humber River for walking and cross-country skiing.

Shopping and dining

  • King Road, King City: Main retail strip between Keele and Dufferin streets.
  • Nobleton plazas: Newer plazas on King Road with everyday shopping.
  • Schomberg Main Street: Historic main street with specialty shops and cafés.

Getting around

King City is about 40 km north of downtown Toronto, with Highway 400 at King Road (exit 43). King City GO Station on the Barrie line is the township's train station, and Metrolinx is adding a second platform, a pedestrian bridge and more parking for future all-day service. GO buses serve Nobleton and King City.

What is changing

  • King City GO Station upgrades: New west platform, pedestrian bridge and parking as part of Barrie line GO expansion.

School boundaries change; confirm your address with the school board before you buy.

King City & Nobleton questions, answered

Is King City & Nobleton a good place to raise a family?+

Yes. King City and Nobleton each have public and Catholic elementary schools, King City Secondary serves the whole township, and private options include Villanova College and The Country Day School. The township also has over 100 km of public trails.

How do I get to downtown Toronto from King City & Nobleton?+

Drive south on Highway 400 from King Road, or take the Barrie line GO train from King City GO to Union Station.
