Living in Maple

Schools

  • Maple Creek Public School: Public (York Region DSB), JK to Grade 8, on Hawker Road.
  • Mackenzie Glen Public School: Public (York Region DSB) elementary school.
  • St. David Catholic Elementary School: Catholic (York Catholic DSB) elementary school.
  • Father John Kelly Catholic Elementary School: Catholic (York Catholic DSB) elementary school.
  • Maple High School: Public (York Region DSB) secondary school.
  • St. Joan of Arc Catholic High School: Catholic (York Catholic DSB) secondary school.

Parks and recreation

  • Maple Community Centre and Library: On Keele Street, with a pool, arena, gym and library, renovated in 2022.
  • Canada's Wonderland: Amusement park on the south edge of Maple.
  • Eagle's Nest Golf Club: Public golf course built on the former Keele Valley landfill.

Shopping and dining

  • Maple village core: Local shops and services around Keele Street and Major Mackenzie Drive.
  • Vaughan Mills: Large indoor mall at Highway 400 and Rutherford Road.
  • Promenade Shopping Centre: Mall serving Maple and Thornhill.

Getting around

Highway 400 runs through Maple with interchanges at Major Mackenzie Drive and Rutherford Road. Maple GO Station on Station Street is on the Barrie line, with trains about every 15 to 30 minutes in the morning rush. YRT routes 26 Maple, 20 Jane, 107 Keele, 85 Rutherford and 4 Major Mackenzie serve the area, and route 26 connects to Vaughan Metropolitan Centre on TTC Line 1.

What is changing

  • Barrie line expansion at Maple GO: Metrolinx is adding a second track, new platforms and more parking toward all-day, two-way GO service.

School boundaries change; confirm your address with the school board before you buy.

Maple questions, answered

Is Maple a good place to raise a family?+

Yes. Maple has public and Catholic elementary and high schools, the Maple Community Centre and Library with a pool and arena, and Canada's Wonderland next door. Highway 400 and Maple GO make commuting easy for working parents.

How do I get to downtown Toronto from Maple?+

By car, take Highway 400 south from Major Mackenzie Drive or Rutherford Road. By transit, take the Barrie line GO train from Maple GO to Union Station, or YRT route 26 to Vaughan Metropolitan Centre and TTC Line 1 downtown.
