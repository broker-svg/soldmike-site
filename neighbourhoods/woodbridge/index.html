Living in Woodbridge

Schools

  • Woodbridge Public School: Public (York Region DSB) elementary school.
  • Pine Grove Public School: Public (York Region DSB) elementary school.
  • St. Margaret Mary Catholic Elementary School: Catholic (York Catholic DSB) elementary school in western Woodbridge.
  • Emily Carr Secondary School: Public high school on Rutherford Road, offering French and Italian courses.
  • Holy Cross Catholic Academy: Catholic high school on Martin Grove Road and an IB World School.
  • Woodbridge College: Public high school in the Woodbridge core.

Parks and recreation

  • Boyd Conservation Park: TRCA park on Islington Avenue for picnics and hiking along the Humber.
  • Kortright Centre for Conservation: 325 hectares of woodland and trails on Pine Valley Drive.
  • Woodbridge Pool and Memorial Arena: Indoor arena, pool and community hall on Highway 7.
  • Woodbridge Fairgrounds: Home of the Woodbridge Fall Fair since 1847.

Shopping and dining

  • Woodbridge Avenue: The traditional downtown, with cafés, restaurants and shops between Islington and Kipling.
  • Market Lane Shopping Centre: Local shops, services and food markets on Woodbridge Avenue.
  • Vaughan Mills: Large indoor mall near Highway 400, a short drive east.

Getting around

Woodbridge sits between Highway 400 and Highway 27, with Highway 7 running through it and Highway 407 just south. The closest GO trains are Rutherford and Maple on the Barrie line, and GO buses also serve the area. YRT routes connect to Vaughan Metropolitan Centre and Pioneer Village stations on TTC Line 1.

What is changing

  • Barrie line GO expansion: Metrolinx is adding a second track and station upgrades at Maple GO toward all-day, two-way service.
  • Highway 427 extension and Highway 413: Ontario is moving ahead in 2026 with extending Highway 427 north and building the Highway 413 east section.
  • Cortellucci Vaughan Hospital: Opened in 2021 on Major Mackenzie Drive, Vaughan's first hospital.

School boundaries change; confirm your address with the school board before you buy.

Woodbridge questions, answered

Is Woodbridge a good place to raise a family?+

Yes. Woodbridge has many public and Catholic schools, including Holy Cross Catholic Academy, an IB World School, plus Boyd Conservation Park, the Kortright Centre and the Woodbridge Pool and Memorial Arena close to home. Woodbridge Avenue and Market Lane cover everyday shopping.

How do I get to downtown Toronto from Woodbridge?+

By car, take Highway 400 or Highway 427 south toward downtown Toronto. By transit, take YRT to Vaughan Metropolitan Centre or Pioneer Village and ride TTC Line 1, or take the Barrie line GO train from Rutherford or Maple.
