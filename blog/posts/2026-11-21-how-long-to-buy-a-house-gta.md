"How long will this take?" comes right after "how much do I need?" for almost every buyer we meet. You want to know when to give notice, when to book movers and when the kids change schools.

**The short answer:** most buyers take **one to three months** to find the right home, then **30 to 90 days** to close. A firm deal can close in **about 30 days** if your lender and lawyer are ready.

Here's where that time goes, and how to keep it from stretching.

## Stage 1: Getting ready (before you look)

This part is up to you, and doing it first saves time later.

- **Know your why.** More space, a better school, a shorter commute. Your reason decides what to look for and what to skip.
- **Get pre-approved.** A lender confirms what you can borrow and holds a rate. It also tells you your real price range, since the [mortgage stress test](/blog/mortgage-stress-test-explained/) qualifies you at a higher rate than the one you'll pay.
- **Know your full number.** Plan for closing costs of about **2%** of the price on top of your down payment. Our [closing costs post](/blog/closing-costs-buyers-vaughan/) walks through it.
- **Have your deposit ready.** In the GTA about **5% of the price** is common, usually due **within 24 hours** of acceptance.

## Stage 2: Finding the home (one to three months)

This is the stretch that varies most.

- **Choose your neighbourhood.** Compare prices, schools, commute and resale value street by street. Our [neighbourhood pages](/neighbourhoods/) are a good place to start.
- **Tour homes.** See them in person. Look past the staging at layout, light, roof, windows and the furnace age.
- **Check the value.** We pull recent sales nearby so you know what a home is really worth before you write.

Being clear on what you want, and pre-approved for it, helps keep this stage short.

## Stage 3: Offer to firm deal

Once you find the home, things move fast.

- **Write the offer:** price, deposit, conditions and closing date.
- **Negotiate.** If there's an offer date, every buyer sends their best offer by that date.
- **Submit your deposit** within about 24 hours of acceptance.
- **Clear your conditions:** financing, home inspection, and a status certificate for condos. Each one has a deadline.

A home inspection usually takes **two to three hours** and costs **$400 to $700** in the GTA. When there's an offer date, buyers often inspect before it so they can make a firm offer. Our [offer nights post](/blog/offer-nights-explained/) covers how to prepare.

Remember: resale homes in Ontario have **no cooling-off period**. Once the deal is firm, you're committed.

## Stage 4: Firm to closing (30 to 90 days)

The closing date is one of the terms you negotiate, so you have some say here.

- **Lawyer and insurance.** Your lawyer searches title and prepares the closing. Home insurance has to be in place before closing day.
- **Final walkthrough.** See the home again before closing to confirm everything is as agreed.
- **Closing day.** Funds move through the lawyers, the deed registers, and you pick up the keys.

About 30 days is possible when your lender and lawyer are ready. You can ask for a longer close to line up with a lease ending or the sale of your current home.

## Selling at the same time?

If you own now, the timeline is really two timelines. In most cases we suggest selling first, or lining up both closings for the same day. Selling first tells you exactly how much you can spend and avoids carrying two homes. Buying first gives you time to find the right home but can force a rushed sale. If you do buy first, ask your lender about a bridge loan.

## Buying pre-construction?

That's a different clock. Resale lets you move in within months. Pre-construction closings can be delayed for years. If you need a home soon, resale usually fits better. More in our [pre-construction or resale post](/blog/pre-construction-or-resale/).

## What this means for you

- **Plan for both clocks:** one to three months to find the home, then 30 to 90 days to close.
- **Get pre-approved first.** It saves time and makes your offers stronger.
- **Book your inspection early** if there's an offer date.
- **Pick a closing date that fits your life,** not just the seller's.
- **Selling too?** Plan both moves together.
