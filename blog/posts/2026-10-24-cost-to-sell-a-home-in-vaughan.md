Your sale price isn't what lands in your bank account. Before you list, it pays to know **every cost of selling**, so you know your real number: what you walk away with.

Here's the full list for a home in Vaughan.

## 1. Real estate commission

Commission is the biggest cost of selling. It's agreed with your brokerage before you list, and it's paid from the sale proceeds on closing, so it doesn't come out of your pocket up front.

## 2. HST on the commission

In Ontario, **13% HST is charged on the commission**. It's easy to forget. Every **$10,000** of commission adds **$1,300** of HST.

## 3. Legal fees

You need a real estate lawyer to sell. Your lawyer transfers the title to the buyer, pays out your mortgage from the sale proceeds and handles the closing funds. Hire one as soon as your deal is firm.

## 4. Paying out your mortgage

Your remaining mortgage is paid off on closing day from the sale proceeds. The cost people miss is the **prepayment penalty**: if you break your mortgage before the end of its term, your lender may charge one. **Ask your lender for the number before you list.**

## 5. Getting the home ready

This one's up to you, and it often pays for itself.

- **Paint, lighting and small repairs return the most.** Fresh neutral paint, bright modern light fixtures, fixing leaky taps, cracked tiles and worn caulking, and a tidy front yard cost little and change how buyers see the home.
- **Staging** ranges from **a few hundred dollars** for a consultation using your own furniture to **several thousand** for a vacant home.
- **Big projects**, like a new kitchen, rarely pay back their full cost right before a sale.

## What you don't pay

- **Land transfer tax.** In Ontario, the **buyer pays it**, not the seller.
- **Capital gains tax on your home, usually.** The principal residence exemption generally covers the gain on the home you live in. Investment and rental properties are taxable, so talk to your accountant before you sell one.

## Work out your net

Here's the simple version:

- Start with your **sale price**
- Minus **commission**
- Minus **13% HST** on the commission
- Minus **legal fees**
- Minus your **mortgage payout** (and any penalty)
- Minus **prep and staging**
- **= what you walk away with**

Every cost on this list is small next to the **sale price** itself. Pricing from real recent sales, a home that shows well, and a strong launch week do more for your net than anything else.
