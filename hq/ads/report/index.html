When to wait, cut, grow or refresh

WaitFirst 3 days, or under about $30 spent. Meta is still learning. Don't touch it.
CutSpent twice your average cost per lead with no leads, or still over twice the average after 7 days.
GrowUnder average for 5–7 days in a row. Raise the budget 20–30%, then wait 3 days.
RefreshPeople see it more than 3 times each, or the tap rate falls 3 days in a row. New photo or video.
Fix the pagePlenty of taps and page visits, few sign-ups. The ad is fine; the page loses them.
Check Follow Up BossMeta counts more leads than Follow Up Boss got. Find the missing person before blaming the ad.
